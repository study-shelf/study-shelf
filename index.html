StudyShelf

A study focus app for iPhone. Plan study blocks, lock distracting apps while you work, and watch every minute turn into pages on your shelf.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email studyshelfs@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Common questions

Apps aren't locking. Open StudyShelf → Settings and check that Screen Time is allowed and that you picked apps to lock. Changes to the locked apps apply from the next day.

The topic check says it needs Apple Intelligence. Topic checks and time estimates use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Without it, proof still works by checking for new written work.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. StudyShelf has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
