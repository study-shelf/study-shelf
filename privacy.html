StudyShelf privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

StudyShelf is a study focus app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

StudyShelf doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you put into the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your study plan: study blocks, topics, deadlines, your daily goal and your history of sessions.
  • Your progress: pages, books, credits, your streak and streak freezes.
  • Your settings.

These are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. They're included in your iPhone backups, like any app's data, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Screen Time

StudyShelf uses Apple's Screen Time to lock the apps you choose during study blocks. Apple doesn't tell StudyShelf which apps you picked; the app only gets a code it passes back to iOS. You can turn off StudyShelf's Screen Time permission any time in the Settings app.

Camera and photos

When you finish a session with proof, StudyShelf takes a photo of your work. Your iPhone reads the text in it and checks that it's new work. With Apple Intelligence it also checks that the text matches your study topic, on your iPhone. The photo is never saved, never sent anywhere, and is gone once the check is done. To spot repeated photos, the app keeps short codes calculated from the text for 60 days. The text itself is not kept.

Calendar

Calendar access is optional. If you allow it:

  • StudyShelf reads when you're busy, so study blocks fit around your events. It only uses the times, not the titles or details of your events.
  • If you turn on writing, StudyShelf adds your study blocks and deadlines to its own "StudyShelf" calendar, and reads changes you make there. It never changes or deletes events in your other calendars.

Your calendar may sync through your own account, such as iCloud or Google, as it does for any calendar app. StudyShelf itself sends nothing anywhere.

Notifications and the lock screen

StudyShelf may show notifications, for example when a planned block about a deadline couldn't start, and a countdown on the lock screen during a session. These can show a deadline title or study topic. You can turn notifications off in the Settings app.

Children

StudyShelf is made for students and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
